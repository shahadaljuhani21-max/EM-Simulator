## Original study-alignment guidance

Use the official EMED615 objectives to choose what to teach and assess. For each topic,
organize learning around recognition of immediate threats, clinical presentation,
focused assessment, useful investigations and interpretation, stabilization,
definitive management, complications, reassessment and disposition where applicable.
Use realistic decision-making and clinically meaningful alternatives, rather than isolated trivia.
These are original organizational suggestions, not quotations or claims about the book's exact content.

The designated book is a permanent study reference, not evidence of current clinical safety.
If a lawfully accessible copy or approved excerpt is supplied separately, use it for
high-yield teaching alignment and record exactly what was accessed. Do not invent page
numbers, chapter claims or citations to unseen text.

## Required source order and limitations

1. Official objectives: mandatory scope and explicit case/decision mapping.
2. Current authoritative medical guidance: mandatory safety verification, including
   doses, contraindications, thresholds, treatment priorities and disposition.
3. First Aid: designated study alignment; only claim content verification if actually accessed.
4. Lecture slides/instructor material: optional course emphasis, never a generation prerequisite.

The 2016 publication date means recommendations may be outdated. Current applicable
guidance takes precedence over outdated study material. Keep medical fact-checking,
critical-management/dose rechecking and independent citation verification separate.
Report unavailable source access and conflicts honestly in metadata.sourceNotes.
Generating without the textbook or slides is allowed; generating without official
objectives is not. Do not fabricate medical verification when current source access is unavailable.
