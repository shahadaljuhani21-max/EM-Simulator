# Official Emergency Medicine course objectives

EMED615 Emergency Course — official 2025 blueprint

Source: user-supplied 2025 Master Exam Blueprint. Staff names and administrative details are omitted.

## Normalization notes
- Stable mapping IDs were assigned by the simulator; they were not printed in the original blueprint.
- Source rows 12 and 13 repeat Cardiogenic Shock with identical objectives; combined into topic T12.
- Other wording, broad topic groups and possible source typos are preserved, not silently corrected.
- Some source paragraphs combine multiple skills; coverage requires addressing the whole objective.
- 38 distinct topic groups imply 76 cases for a full-course pack, two per group.

## Course learning outcomes
- **EMED615-CLO-1-1**: To explain the pathophysiology and presentation of life/organ-threatening diseases.
- **EMED615-CLO-1-2**: To develop a general approach to medical and surgical emergencies.
- **EMED615-CLO-1-3**: To analyze common emergency situations and demonstrate how to manage them.
- **EMED615-CLO-2-1**: Demonstrates behavior that conveys caring, honesty, genuine interest, and tolerance when interacting with a diverse population.
- **EMED615-CLO-2-2**: Demonstrates behavior that conveys punctuality with acknowledgment of the value of time and conveys professional behavior in the emergency department.
- **EMED615-CLO-3-1**: To organize a general approach to medical and surgical emergencies
- **EMED615-CLO-3-2**: To perform common emergency procedures.
